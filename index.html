rclone

This page is the home page of a small personal Google OAuth client named rclone.

It lets one person use the open-source command-line program rclone on their own computer to copy and organise files in their own Google Drive. It is not a public service, it has no website or server of its own, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

When the owner runs rclone, the program signs in to the owner's Google account and reads, creates or deletes files in the owner's own Google Drive, only when the owner asks it to. All of this happens on the owner's own computer.

See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
