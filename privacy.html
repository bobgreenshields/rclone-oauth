rclone: privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

This policy covers the personal Google OAuth client named rclone. The client is used by its owner alone, with the open-source program rclone, to access the owner's own Google Drive.

What data is accessed

When the owner signs in, Google asks for permission to see, edit, create and delete files in the owner's Google Drive. rclone then reads and changes only the files the owner asks it to, when the owner runs a command.

What is collected, stored or shared

Nothing is collected by the developer. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. The files, and the sign-in token that Google issues to rclone, stay on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove this app's access at any time in their Google Account, under Security, in the list of third-party apps and services with access to the account.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the Issues tab of the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
